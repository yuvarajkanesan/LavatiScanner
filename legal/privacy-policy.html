Privacy Policy

Lavati Scanner · Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Lavati Scanner ("the App") accesses, how it's used, and what control you have over it. The short version: your scans stay on your device unless you explicitly turn on Google Drive backup, and we (the developer) never receive or see your documents.

1. What the App accesses

Permission / dataWhy
CameraTo capture documents, ID cards, and other images you choose to scan.
VibrationHaptic feedback on capture (e.g. the shutter button).
InternetOnly used when you choose to sign in and back up to Google Drive (see below).
StorageYour scans, their SQLite index, and exported files are stored in the App's private storage on your device.

2. Where your content lives

Every document, page image, and OCR result you create is processed and stored entirely on your device. Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device via Google's ML Kit library — no image or text is sent to a server to be recognized. The App has no backend server of its own and does not transmit your documents anywhere by default.

3. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to turn on Drive backup in Settings, the App will:

  • Ask you to sign in with your Google account.
  • Request only the drive.file permission scope — this lets the App see and manage only the files it creates in your Drive. It cannot see, read, or modify any other file already in your Google Drive.
  • Upload a PDF copy of your documents into a single "Lavati Scanner" folder it creates in your own Drive. Nothing is sent to us — the upload goes directly from your device to your Google account.

You can turn this off, or revoke the App's Drive access entirely, at any time from Settings in the App or from your Google Account permissions page. Turning it off does not delete files already uploaded to Drive — those are managed like any other file in your Drive.

4. What we don't do

  • No ads or ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your content or personal data with third parties.
  • We don't have a server that stores or has access to your scans.

5. Data retention & deletion

Deleting a document in the App deletes it from your device immediately. If that document was backed up to Drive, deleting it in the App also removes it from your Drive folder. Uninstalling the App removes all of its locally stored data. Revoking Drive access (section 3) stops any further uploads but does not retroactively delete files already in your Drive.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Your scans are stored in the App's private, sandboxed storage area, which other apps cannot access. Any data sent to Google's Drive API during an optional backup is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection using Google's own sign-in and authorization flow.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer contact listed on the Lavati Scanner Play Store listing.
